Grid Guide Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Grid Guide does not collect any data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your Mac, and nothing is shared with anyone.

What the app does

Grid Guide is a macOS menu bar utility that draws a click-through grid overlay on your displays. It only draws on top of the screen. It never reads, records or captures what is on your screen.

Data collection

  • No personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, identifiers or location are collected.
  • The app makes no network connections and contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or tracking.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

Data stored on your Mac

Your layouts, appearance settings and keyboard shortcuts are saved locally in the app's sandboxed preferences on your Mac so they are there the next time you open it. This data is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

Grid Guide does not ask for Accessibility, Screen Recording, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Files or any other permission. The optional "Launch at login" setting uses the standard macOS login item service, which you can turn off in the app or in System Settings › General › Login Items.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
